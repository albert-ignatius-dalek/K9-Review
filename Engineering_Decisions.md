# Engineering Decisions Log

A running record of major decisions and why they were made — curated from
the private engineering history, not a full transcript.

## 2026-09-06 — Volume-first generation replaces author-then-check

**Decision**: structure for any subsystem sharing space with a moving
part is generated by boolean intersection with a validated motion
envelope, not authored freely and checked against one afterward.

**Why**: three hand-authored pelvis candidates (torque box, semi-
monocoque, space-frame) all intruded into the real sitting-pose leg
envelope (32.9k–50.7k mm³). Rather than trim material reactively, the
workflow inverted: `Body Volume − Motion Envelope − Bearing Envelope −
Servo Envelope = Available Structural Volume`, and structure is generated
inside that bound. Validated on Mechanical Pelvis Alpha: identical
structural shape to Candidate A, but 28.7 cm³ of envelope intrusion vs.
54.6 cm³ — the improvement is attributable to generation order alone.

## 2026-09-06 — Rear Drive Module renamed to Mechanical Pelvis Assembly

**Why**: "Rear Drive Module" frames the subsystem around motors and
structure. "Mechanical Pelvis" frames it around anatomy, movement,
packaging, and structure simultaneously — the renaming is meant to keep
anatomy-first thinking the default, not a cosmetic change.

## 2026-09-06 — Sitting posture is a design choice, not a biology lookup

**Why**: Mark 6 is not anatomically identical to a dog, so exact canine
joint-angle replication isn't the objective. The method is: define the
desired posture, then solve the mechanism backward — bounded by passive
biological safety-stop limits (never commanded to the limit itself), not
derived from them.

## 2026-09-06 — Candidate C (space-frame) stays under consideration

**Why**: it has the lowest total material of the three candidates for a
similar motion-envelope intrusion. It lacks a working stiffness
evaluation method (Bredt-Batho's closed-thin-wall theory doesn't apply to
an open space-frame), but a missing evaluation method is not the same as
a bad architecture — it stays open pending a method that actually fits
the topology.

## 2026-09-06 — Character direction: deliberate movement, not mechanical limitation

**Decision**: Mark 6 represents K9 after thousands of years of
accumulated experience — calm, economical, purposeful movement by choice,
with full capability for rapid decisive action when genuinely needed.
Not aged or worn down. This is a standing constraint on future
gait/motion-planning work, not just a narrative note.

## 2026-09-06 — Battery capacity confirmed at 5000mAh

**Why**: an earlier informal mention of "6000mAh" was superseded by
direct confirmation, consistent with a 2026-07-23 project log ("12V 3S2P
5000mAh Li-ion, Jon confirmed"). Physical dimensions, connector, and BMS
status remain unmeasured — the spec being confirmed doesn't mean the
part is fully characterized yet.
